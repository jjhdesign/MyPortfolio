Title: GYEONGCHUN-LINE FOREST PARK Brand Identity
Category: BRANDING
Date: 2025.09 - 2025.12
Contribution: Research 30% / Design 100%
Type: Identity Design(2) Class
Color: #161616
===
GYEONGCHUN-LINE FOREST PARK is a 5.4km linear park created through the regeneration of a former railway line. Although it has become a widely visited public space, a clear identity and consistent user experience have not yet been fully established. This project proposes a new brand identity to strengthen the park’s character and enhance the visitor experience.
