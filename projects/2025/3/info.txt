Title: Snip: Think in a snap
Category: UI/UX
Year: 2025
Contribution: Research&Design 25%
Client: 60th KOREA INTERNATIONAL DESIGN AWARD
Color: #1a1a1a
===
Snip is a new media literacy-based thinking platform that helps users ask, interpret, and express—starting with short-form content. For teens used to YouTube Shorts or Reels, Snip shifts the habit from passive scrolling to active thinking. Its three-step flow—Explore, Analyze, and Express—guides users from curiosity to insight and clear communication. In the “Lounge,” users exchange perspectives and reinterpret content creatively. Features like “Relay” and “Remix” enable chain-writing, adding depth and collaboration to thinking.
